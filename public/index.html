No upload · No storage · No analytics · No training

Processing happens locally in your browser. The shipped application does not transmit or persist your input, and contains no analytics, telemetry, or training-data collection.

Generate, preview, copy, review-package creation, and ZIP export run locally. “Review with Your AI” only copies a review package to your clipboard; the tool does not send it to an AI service.

If you choose to paste that package into another service, that service’s data policy applies. This boundary does not cover browser extensions, a compromised operating system, user-installed third-party modifications, or a service into which you manually paste content.

State is held in memory only. Nothing is written to your filesystem or browser storage unless you explicitly download the generated ZIP.
